CODE4 METHODS AND INTERPRETATION

Scientific question
How much does the observed candidate-gene coverage differ from the coverage
of annotated noncandidate genes under alternative matching definitions?
This is not an assessment of aroma-effect replication or breeding value.

Fixed elements
Retain both original target sets, the eligible Criollo v2 annotation catalog,
all original candidate exclusions, the three marker resources and A/B/C
coordinate states, and every original flank distance. A zero distance means
that a marker lies inside a closed gene interval; flanks are inclusive.
Do not change target genes to make a matching model feasible.

Matching sensitivity
The length-only comparator matches chromosome, gene biotype and length bin.
The density comparator additionally matches local annotated-gene density bin.
The additional grid is a revision sensitivity grid, not a reconstruction of
an originally prespecified optimization. The original three density bins and
+/-250-kb neighborhood are retained as the reference configuration; no claim
is made that these are biologically optimal. Coarser/finer bins and narrower/
wider neighborhoods examine dependence on those choices.

The neighborhood counts all eligible annotated gene midpoints on the same
chromosome, excluding only the focal gene itself. It is not the number of
markers and does not condition directly on the coverage outcome.
Quantile boundaries are determined separately by biotype from noncandidate
genes. Equal feature values are assigned consistently to the same bin.
When a stratum lacks enough noncandidate genes, the whole target/model pair
remains blocked. No replacement, stratum widening or target omission occurs.

Empirical percentiles
For observed covered-gene count O and R background draws with counts B_r:
  strict   = 100 * number(B_r < O) / R
  inclusive= 100 * number(B_r <= O) / R
  midrank  = 100 * [number(B_r < O) + 0.5 * number(B_r = O)] / R
These use integer covered-gene counts to avoid float-based tie ambiguity.
The strict-to-inclusive interval exposes discrete ties. Midrank is the
primary descriptive percentile and the other two values remain available.
The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of draw coverage use linear interpolation.
They describe variation among draws, not a confidence interval for a trait
effect. No P-values, FDR correction or significance labels are generated.

Ascertainment
The candidate list was assembled around published associations, including
those detected with the G7 and MAF5 exports. Coverage by those same resources
is therefore partly conditioned on discovery. Diversity provides another
marker-resource comparison, but shared marker/sequence provenance means it
is not an independent biological replication. Matching annotated genes does
not reconstruct the original tested-marker universe, remove candidate-
selection effects, or jointly control marker counts and spatial dependence.

Reporting
Report the full sensitivity grid, including unavailable configurations.
Do not select a matching model because it puts observed coverage inside a
particular interval. Distinguish target sets, coordinate states, radii and
coverage flanks in tables and prose. If conclusions vary, report that
dependence directly rather than describing one model as definitive.
