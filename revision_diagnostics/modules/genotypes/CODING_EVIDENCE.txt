Genotype-coding evidence and limits
Evidence reviewed 26 October 2026

DECISION
FORMAT_COMPATIBLE_CONDITIONAL_DOSAGE
The working mapping is A=0, M=1, C=2. Results are conditional on M representing
the heterozygous state between two homozygous export states. The exact exporter
codebook, reference/alternate polarity and favorable aroma allele remain
unconfirmed.

DIRECT EVIDENCE FROM THE PINNED WORKBOOKS
Both Nacional files are BIFF8 XLS workbooks with first five columns:
marker_id, alleles, chrom, pos, strand; sample columns follow immediately.
G7 contains 4,540 markers and 152 samples; every allele cell is A>C.
MAF5 contains 6,341 markers and 153 samples; 4,273 allele cells are A/C and
2,068 are C/A. In both matrices the observed genotype symbols are A, M and C.
These source observations are reported in the genotype-audit tables.

OFFICIAL FORMAT DOCUMENTATION
TASSEL 5's nucleotide-code appendix maps A to A:A, C to C:C, and M to A:C.
This supports the compatibility of the conditional three-state interpretation:
https://tassel.maizegenetics.net/user_manual/appendix/nucleotidecodes/

The TASSEL loading guide describes standard HapMap files as having eleven
marker-metadata columns before sample values. These XLS exports have five.
Therefore, their abbreviated layout is not an exact standard HapMap match;
format similarity alone does not establish the provenance of their encoding:
https://tassel.maizegenetics.net/user_manual/load/

RELATED PRIMARY STUDY
Colonges et al. (2021), Two Main Biosynthesis Pathways Involved in the Synthesis
of the Floral Aroma of the Nacional Cocoa Variety, Frontiers in Plant Science.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2021.681979
Full text: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2021.681979/full

The study reports DArTseq SNP genotyping, analysis in TASSEL v5, G7 and MAF5
filtering, and the same named TropGene Nacional aroma collection. This is
supporting provenance, not proof that the frozen exports are identical to the
paper's analysis sets: its reported marker counts differ. Figure 1 identifies
SNA604 as an ancestral Nacional control. The primary analysis retains this
sample; its omission is evaluated separately as a sensitivity analysis.

WHAT THE QC CAN AND CANNOT ESTABLISH
Literal frequencies and between-export concordance can verify internal
consistency. Conditional per-sample and per-marker M fractions and 2p(1-p)
can describe the data, but cannot independently prove the coding semantics.
No Hardy-Weinberg test is used to certify an encoding, and allele declaration
order is not treated as evidence of genome or favorable-allele orientation.

All raw shared-marker disagreements are concentrated in SNA604 and L17H53.
We retain these samples in primary analyses and show their omission separately.
The two exports' matching remaining cells imply matching dosage calculations
on the same retained markers and samples; this is not independent replication.

The pinned inputs contain no per-call read-depth or genotype-likelihood fields.
Missingness or M fractions cannot establish sequencing depth as the cause of
between-export differences. The interpretation is specific to the Nacional
exports and must not be transferred automatically to Amazonia or other panels.

SCOPE OF DOWNSTREAM ANALYSES
Structure and kinship adjustments retain the conditional coding interpretation.
Cross-export analyses use explicit focal/partner identities and shared samples.
Within-panel results describe coordinate anchors; a missing focal genotype is
not evidence of weak LD.
