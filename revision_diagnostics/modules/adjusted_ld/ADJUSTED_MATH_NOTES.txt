Adjusted-correlation mathematical definitions and implementation

Given sample covariance V and nuisance columns C=[intercept,covariates], define
W=V^+ and P=W-W C (C' W C)^+ C' W. Adjusted squared correlation is
(x' P y)^2/[(x' P x)(y' P y)]. The code computes a factor T with P=T T'
by eigendecomposition-based whitening and an orthogonal nuisance projection.
Genotype rows multiply T; squared normalized inner products are evaluated.
They are not centered a second time after transformation.

V=I with an intercept gives ordinary squared Pearson correlation. Genetic PCs
give squared partial correlations. PCs are continuous axes of genetic variation,
not independently measured ancestral mixture proportions. Covariance and joint
versions follow Mangin et al.2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/hdy.2011.73.

KERNEL
Background marker selection and test-chromosome exclusion occur outside this
module. For marker-by-sample dosage D, p=mean(D)/2 and Z=D-2p. The relationship
kernel is Z'Z/[2 sum p(1-p)], then normalized to mean diagonal1. This is a centered
genomic relationship, not a pedigree estimate. PCs use Z/sqrt[2p(1-p)]. Both are
invariant to flipping0 and2 at a marker; biological allele orientation is not
inferred. Any regularization is explicit in the calling module's diagnostics.
References: VanRaden2008, https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2007-0980;
Price et al.2006, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1847.

EXPLICIT DIFFERENCES FROM LDCORSV DEFAULTS
The implementation uses a stated relative eigenvalue tolerance rather than an
absolute 1e-5 cutoff. The minimum sample size is 50; zero residual variance is
recorded as missing rather than zero correlation. These settings differ from
the LDcorSV defaults.

Centered K has the intercept in its null space. The production covariance adds
ridge*I and ones(n,n)/n. The second term affects only the intercept direction;
after fitting an intercept it leaves the residual metric unchanged relative to
K+ridge*I. Equivalence is independently tested with and without PC covariates.
It prevents software eigenvalue thresholds from discarding an intercept direction
whose eigenvalue would otherwise equal a very small ridge.

Missing source values are rejected. The genotype audit found complete A/M/C
calls in the pinned matrices. No mean imputation or zero filling is performed. All dosage
interpretations remain conditional; adjustment does not establish source coding,
focal variant identity, causality, or breeding utility.
